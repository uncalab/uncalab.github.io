How language works, how it breaks down and how it recovers

The Cognitive Neuroscience and Aphasia Unit (UNCA) studies how language works, how it breaks down after brain damage and how it recovers. We work with people with aphasia and with people without brain damage, combining clinical and neuropsychological assessment, experimental tasks and neuroimaging.
